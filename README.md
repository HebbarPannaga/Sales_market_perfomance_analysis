# Sales_market_perfomance_analysis
Performed sales and market performance analysis using SQL, Python, and Power BI to identify key revenue drivers, customer/product concentration, pricing impact, and regional sales trends, enabling data-driven business insights and growth opportunities.

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing 4 years of sales data to understand revenue trends, customer behavior, product performance, pricing impact, regional sales distribution, and channel contribution.  

Using SQL, Python, and Power BI, the analysis was performed to identify key business drivers, uncover sales concentration patterns, evaluate pricing influence on revenue, and generate actionable business insights through interactive dashboards and visualizations.

---

## Analysis Performed
- Revenue trend analysis across multiple years
- Product and customer performance analysis
- Regional and city-wise sales analysis
- Sales channel contribution analysis
- Pricing vs sales volume impact analysis
- Profit margin and customer profitability analysis
- Order value distribution and pricing outlier detection
- Seasonal sales pattern analysis

---

## Key Outcomes
- Identified top-performing product and customer segments contributing ~50–60% of total revenue, highlighting key business drivers and high-value segments

- Discovered that pricing had a greater influence on revenue growth than sales volume, indicating pricing optimization as a major opportunity for business growth

- Found top 5 cities contributing ~50% of overall revenue/profit, revealing strong regional concentration in sales performance

- Identified wholesale channel contributing 50%+ of total sales, highlighting dependency on a single channel and potential diversification opportunities

- Analyzed customer profitability trends and observed stable margins among high-revenue customers, indicating strong pricing consistency across major accounts

- Detected pricing outliers caused by bulk orders, premium bundles, and promotional discounts, helping distinguish regular sales patterns from exceptional transactions
